## Trustindex answer

Its official support article explicitly says there is no public API. Terms §4.E describe general RSS/Atom/XML Feed Content for personal non-commercial display with credit/link and prohibit redistribution; this does not establish a suitable commercial Google-review feed. A partner API or separately licensed Google export was not established by the reviewed pages. Free plugin refresh is manual with the latest ten reviews; paid subscriptions automatically update their own widgets. Buying that subscription does not establish permission to move its data into this plugin. An authorized partner/export agreement and documented endpoint would be required. The screenshot's author/photo/rating/date/text/reply and moderation experience can be compared visually, but private plugin internals, authentication and subscription gates were not inspected or copied.

## Minimum vendor confirmation before a connector

Obtain a named plan/account and a written agreement expressly covering the intended non-owned listing, authentic individual reviews, 100+ where present, supported endpoint/pagination/stable IDs, daily freshness, custom public WordPress rendering, local retention, deletion/revocation, attribution and commercial customer use. Confirm upstream acquisition is permitted, rate limits and costs for each location count. Then supply credentials privately in WordPress, approve any billable test budget and validate Menjar Financial's actual identity and data. An endpoint alone is insufficient. See [integration decision](PHASE5-INTEGRATION-DECISION.md).
